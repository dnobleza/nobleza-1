Status: IDEAS ONLY. Nothing here is on the page. The live copy is still the approved preview copy.
Edit freely, then tell Claude which lines to apply.

## Why this exists
The design critique found two things in the current copy:
1. It is generic. "Digital Growth Partner", "Meet your goals, always" and "Turn your ideas into reality" could belong to any agency. DESIGN.md section 7 asks for plain, specific, friendly copy with no hype words.
2. It never says what CodeHaus actually offers: a client portal where you send a build request, follow its status, and see past work (PRODUCT.md).

## Rules these ideas follow
- Only say what the product really does today. Do not claim timelines, prices, numbers or results.
- PRODUCT.md says request fields and status names are still undecided, so "track your request" stays general (no named stages).
- Plain English a Philippine SMB owner can read quickly. Taglish is an option, but that is your call.
- No hype words (seamless, unlock, supercharge, next-level, revolutionary), no emojis.
- "Start a project" currently goes to /register. Copy near it should not hide that you create an account first.
